A new chapter for Lovely Drinks

Lovely Drinks returns with a simple idea: soft drinks should be as carefully considered as the food and places they are served with.

Clean ingredients. Elegant balance. Thoughtfully produced in Somerset.

❦

The first range

Lightly carbonated and refreshingly balanced, our drinks are crafted using ingredients we grow ourselves or source from the UK wherever possible. Made with all natural ingredients, we use hand-picked wild elderflowers alongside English roses and English Raspberries to create clean, delicate botanical flavours with a true sense of place.

  • Elderflower Presse
  • Elderflower & Rose
  • Raspberry Lemonade
  • Indian Tonic
  • Elderflower Tonic

Available in 250ml & 200ml slimline cans.

❦

From field to drink

Lovely Drinks is rooted in the Somerset countryside, where elderflower and other botanicals will increasingly shape the drinks we make.

Some ingredients will always come from specialist growers further afield, but wherever possible we want to bring flavour closer to the land.

Botanical flavour begins in the field.
