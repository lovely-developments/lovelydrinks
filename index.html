A new chapter for Lovely Drinks

Lovely Drinks returns with a focused range of premium botanical soft drinks — crafted for restaurants, bars and people who appreciate proper flavour.

Clean ingredients. Elegant balance. Thoughtfully produced in Somerset.

❦

The first range

Lightly carbonated and refreshingly balanced, our drinks are crafted using ingredients we grow ourselves or source from the UK wherever possible. Made with all natural ingredients, we use hand-picked wild elderflowers alongside English roses and English lavender to create clean, delicate botanical flavours with a true sense of place.

  • Elderflower Presse
  • Elderflower & Rose
  • Apple & Elderflower
  • Garden Lemonade
  • Indian Tonic
  • Light Tonic
  • Elderflower Tonic

Available in 250ml cans and bottles.

❦

From field to drink

Lovely Drinks is rooted in the Somerset countryside, where elderflower and other botanicals will increasingly shape the drinks we make.

Some ingredients will always come from specialist growers further afield, but wherever possible we want to bring flavour closer to the land.

Botanical flavour begins in the field.
